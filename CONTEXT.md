# Spell finance glossary

- **Subscription** — a recurring commitment with a price, cadence, next renewal date, platform name, and optional project link. It forecasts future commitments; it is not a recorded expense.
- **Transaction** — a dated cash record. Its kind is either **income** (money received) or **expense** (money spent).
- **Project link** — an optional connection from a subscription or transaction to a Spell project. It makes project income, costs, and net value visible without making finance depend on a project.
- **Archive** — a reversible inactive state. Archived subscriptions stop appearing in upcoming renewals; archived transactions remain out of normal reporting but retain their historical record.
- **Workspace currency** — the single currency used to label all finance amounts in one Spell vault. Changing it relabels stored amounts; it does not perform an exchange-rate conversion.
